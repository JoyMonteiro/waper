# 2026-08-07 — baseline carry-forward

**This is a seed entry, not a fresh assessment.** No new figures were read today; the
session was a status review and a plan-archiving pass. What follows reconstructs the
conclusions we had already reached, from the record, so the log starts with them
instead of losing them. Each item cites where it came from. Fresh assessments start
with the next entry.

## Verdict

Identification is in decent shape and just gained a targeted fix for its main known
pathology (over-merging). Tracking is split: the RWP-level tracker works, while the
feature-level tracker is correct but answers a different question than the one we
wanted — which we established the hard way and must not re-litigate.

## Wins

- **Feature matching itself is sound.** In the purple-track diagnostic the tracker
  held IoU **0.84–0.94** with its match at every step right up to termination. When
  it stopped, it stopped because no minimum existed within 21° — the feature had
  physically decayed. That is correct behaviour, not a failure.
  *Source: `project_feature_tracks` memory, 2026-06-19 diagnostic; `scripts/diag_purple.py`.*

- **WAPER is highly precise against the Zimin envelope.** Across 720 timesteps, the
  area WAPER flags that the envelope does *not* is only **0.04** (edge-pruning 0.0–0.01),
  falling to 0.003 at the harshest setting. WAPER is close to a strict subset of the
  envelope — it very rarely invents packets.
  *Source: `results/method_comparison_sweep.csv`.*

- **Detection agreement is perfect through the usable range.** `detection_agreement`
  holds at **1.0** for every threshold up to edge-pruning 0.04 — the two methods always
  agree on *whether* a packet exists; they differ only on extent.
  *Source: same CSV.*

- **The IoU optimum corroborates the candidate operating point.** Mean IoU peaks at
  **0.374** at edge-pruning 0.01 (0.373 at 0.0, 0.369 at 0.02), and the operating-point
  candidate carried in `visualize.py` is GT=0.02 — near the top of the curve.
  Weak evidence, but independent of the sweeps that chose it.
  *Source: same CSV; `datasets/experiments/2026-03-21-initial-audit.md`.*

- **Over-merging now has a shipped fix.** Latitude-gated branch resolution
  (in-band zonal exclusivity + orphan reassignment, `lat_gate=15.0`) is implemented,
  reviewed, and committed. **Its effect on the metrics has never been measured.**

## Open problems

- **[Structural] Feature tracks follow phase, not group velocity.** The eastward
  progression we wanted to capture is the envelope moving at group velocity, carried by
  a *succession of distinct troughs* (199-, 266-, 278-class, each creeping east ~1°/hr
  independently). The purple seed at 308.9°E/43.6°N is near-stationary (~+1°/hr), drifts
  south, and decays by t=11. At the t=1 split the only fragments are the stationary
  primary (IoU 0.42) and one moving *westward* (Δlon −46) — there is no eastward fragment
  to capture. The 30°E trough at t=26 is brand new, with no footprint lineage back to 309°.
  **A feature-track layer cannot express this answer.** Fixing it requires an
  envelope/group-velocity primitive, a trough-to-trough handoff heuristic, or dropping
  the expectation. *This decision is still open and is the main blocker on the
  feature-track line of work.*
  *Source: `project_feature_tracks` memory (re-framed 2026-06-19); `scripts/diag_purple.py`, `scripts/diag_envelope.py`.*

- **[Open] Agreement with the envelope method plateaus at IoU ≈ 0.37.** The gap is
  almost entirely one-directional: envelope-only area is **0.29** against WAPER-only
  0.04. So the ceiling is set by the envelope covering much more area than WAPER, not by
  WAPER over-reaching. Unresolved whether that excess is real packet extent WAPER
  truncates, or envelope diffuseness — which is what the Souders-style T21 spatial +
  24 h temporal filtering in commit `98074b4` was meant to test. **That test's result
  was never recorded.** First thing to re-run.
  *Source: `results/method_comparison_sweep.csv`; commit `98074b4`.*

- **[Open] Aggressive edge pruning collapses the method.** Beyond 0.04, IoU falls off a
  cliff (0.259 → 0.099 → 0.044) and detection agreement breaks down (1.0 → 0.817 →
  0.538 at 0.06 and 0.08). The usable band is narrow: **GT ≲ 0.04**, and the interesting
  region is 0.0–0.02.
  *Source: same CSV.*

- **[Open] `lat_gate` has never been swept.** It is a new tunable (default 15.0) that
  directly controls the over-merging behaviour, and no sensitivity run covers it. It is
  also absent from the parameter list in `datasets/experiments/README.md`.

- **[Housekeeping] The operating point is still not codified.** `visualize.py` hard-codes
  GT=0.02 / penalty=4000 / ST=20 while `WaperConfig` defaults remain GT=3e-5 /
  penalty=2000. Flagged 2026-03-21 as "implicit, not yet codified"; unchanged 4½ months
  later. Any assessment of "current performance" is ambiguous until this is resolved,
  because two different configurations are both live.

## Ruled out

  diagnostic, not by argument: matching quality was 0.84–0.94 throughout, so there is no
  threshold left to loosen. Promoted to the do-not-retry register.
